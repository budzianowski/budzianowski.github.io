I'm a co-founder and CEO of [Lute](https://lute.one), where we build the robotics workforce of the future - if you are interested, reach out!
 
Previously, I co-founded [K-Scale Labs](https://ben.bolte.cc/static/docs/kscale-whitepaper.pdf) where we produced affordable and open-source humanoid robots to democratize embodied AI. I also created and co-lectured the joint NLP course for ML Master's programs at [Warsaw University](https://mimuw.edu.pl/en/studies/machine-learning-a-new-field-of-masters-programs/) and [Jagiellonian University](https://gmum.net/). I am passionate about everything that is related to applied mathematics, statistics, machine learning and artificial intelligence.

I used to lead the ML team at [PolyAI](https://poly.ai/) working on conversational AI in research and production. I did my PhD in Engineering Department(https://divf.eng.cam.ac.uk/) at Cambridge University working on long-term conversational agents jointly supervised by [prof. Rich Turner](https://learning.eng.cam.ac.uk/Public/Turner/WebHome) and [prof. Anna Korhonen](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~alk23/). I also had a chance to work with [prof. Milica Gašić](https://www.cs.hhu.de/en/research-groups/dialog-systems-and-machine-learning.html).

Prior to that, I was working on training deep belief networks using high-temperature expansion being supervised by [prof. Rich Turner](https://learning.eng.cam.ac.uk/Public/Turner/WebHome). For my bachelor, I graduated in mathematics from [Adam Mickiewicz University](https://wmi.amu.edu.pl/en).

If you have any questions feel free to contact me - my mail is budzianowski at gmail dot com.
